FAQ

Common questions about GPT Car

Can a chat be handed to a live agent?

Yes. Any scenario can end by handing the conversation to your chat or CRM.

Does it integrate with our CRM and back-office systems?

Yes. We connect the bot to your CRM, databases and internal systems via API — leads, bookings and chat history land where you already work. The scope and depth of integrations is scoped to your infrastructure.

What about customer data security?

Data travels over secure channels and is used only to run your bot. Access is scoped, and what's stored and how it's processed is agreed to match your requirements and regulations.

Who's responsible for the accuracy of answers?

The bot answers strictly within your knowledge base and defined rules. Complex or sensitive questions go to an agent, and you can narrow, review and adjust its boundaries — you fully control the tone and scope.

How do we bring customers to the bot?

Driving traffic to the bot is the dealer's part. You can:

  • Post about the bot on social media and your website
  • Run an email or SMS campaign to customers
  • Train your team to recommend the bot to customers

Customers quickly realise the bot is the most convenient channel. We can also help promote it in relevant owner groups, where the bot gathers brand experience.

Why would owners actually use this bot?

It answers faster than an agent, factors in the model, VIN and context, and leads straight to a useful next step.

Can we run it across multiple brands?

Yes. You can launch a single multi-brand bot, or separate bots per line.

How long does launch take?

A light-mode pilot goes live in 3–5 days. Full mode depends on the integrations.

Which channels are supported?

WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, a website widget, social media and other operator chat platforms.

Can it use our own documents?

Yes. The bot learns from your PDFs, Word, Excel, internal policies and catalogues.
